Rules (para sa Claude Code)
  • Two users only: PWD signs (no typing on the sign screen); non-PWD speaks or types (existing screen).
  • After every Send → “Whose turn is it?” (existing, unchanged).
  • Shortcut inside a turn: Sign screen has “🗣 Speak or Type”; Speak/Type screen has “🤟 Sign instead”. One tap goes straight to the other turn. An unsent signed sentence is kept until the PWD comes back, Sends, or taps + New conversation.
  • PWD Send: message added at once and played automatically (per-sign recorded audio → browser voice for signs without audio). 🔊 on each signed message replays; tap again stops.
  • Non-PWD Translate → avatar fills the whole area under the top bar, caption with the current word/letter highlighted, Replay / Edit / Send. Send adds the message directly; the “You said … → Add to Conversation / Try again” popup is removed.
  • Avatar only: a word with avatar data plays its sign; a word without it is fingerspelled by the avatar using the letter signs (a–z) and number signs (1–10, other digits spelled one by one; a character with no sign shows as text in the caption). The old video playback (assets/signs/**/*.webm, fallback.png) is removed from Let's Talk, Learn Signs and Quiz.
  • Audio per sign is part of the sign data (see sign-audio-wireframe.html screen 1: Train Gestures → Audio section).
