STEP 1 OF 4

Step 1: Name and Capture

Choose the sign set, type the English and/or Filipino text, then record 3 samples with the camera.

  • 1Choose the sign set: ESL, FSL, or Both.
  • 2Type the English text and/or the Filipino text for the sign (the fields shown depend on the set).
  • 3Press Record 3 samples, sign the gesture 3 times with a rest between, then review and save.
STEP 2 OF 4

Step 2: Compile the Model

Compile builds the recognition model file from every saved sample. Steps 3 and 4 and Let's Talk use that file. Keep this screen open until Compile is complete.

  • 1Choose the model: ESL (ESL and Both signs) or FSL (FSL and Both signs).
  • 2Press Compile when the status line says the model is out of date.
  • 3Wait for the progress bar to finish before continuing.
STEP 3 OF 4

Step 3: Predict Gesture

Test the trained model live with your camera before translating. The prediction result appears in the panel.

  • 1Enable the camera if not already active.
  • 2Perform your gesture in front of the camera.
  • 3Continue when the confidence looks stable.
STEP 4 OF 4

Step 4: Translate

Perform your Start gesture to add a word. Stop gesture marks a pause. Words are spoken aloud!

  • 1Perform the Start gesture to add a word to your translation.
  • 2Perform the Stop gesture to mark a pause.
  • 3Speak, copy, or save your translation when done.
